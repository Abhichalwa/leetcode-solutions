## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used a brute-force approach by checking every pair of elements in the array. If the sum of two elements is equal to the target, their indices are returned.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

The solution checks all possible pairs and works when the matching elements are at different positions in the array.
